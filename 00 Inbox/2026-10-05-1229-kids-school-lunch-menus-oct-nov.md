# Kids' school hot lunches: October–November 2026

Kids eat at school on **Mon, Tue, Thu** only. Menus below are those days only, from 5 Oct. Dish names verbatim (French).

## Logistics
- Hot meals are **by advance reservation only**, on the school's online parent platform ("Réservations de repas").
- **November: book by Fri 16 Oct 2026 (inclusive).** Bookings can be cancelled until then. Missed deadline = no hot meal.
- The child's platform account must be **topped up before booking**; a top-up takes a few days to show.
- Absence refund **only with a medical certificate**; cancel by email to the school's economat **by 11:00 on the day**.
- October booking closed 23 Sep. October menu runs to Thu 15 Oct (nothing listed 19–30 Oct). **No school Mon 2 Nov** (confirmed by David).
