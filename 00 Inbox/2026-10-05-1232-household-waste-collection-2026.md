## Rules
- Put out **before 06:00** on collection day, or the evening before **after 18:00**. Jun–Aug: before 05:00 (heat).
- Kerbside, visible, not blocking the path; residual, organic and paper bags off the ground when it rains.
- **No plastic containers** for residual, organic or paper bags, or PMC (damaged containers not their responsibility).
- Max **15 kg** per bag / paper box. Bags closed so they can be grabbed from the top, **no tape**.
- Sharp waste in the residual bag: wrap in newspaper.
- Snow/ice: if missed, take it back in and put out at the next collection of the same type.

## Other services
- **Bulky items:** free pickup at home by appointment (city's reuse centre). Book ahead, there's a waiting time.
- **Christmas trees** (natural, no stand, no decoration): our zone 11 Jan, before 08:00 on the pavement. 2027 date: check the next calendar.
- **Recycling park:** toy collection **Sat 10 Oct 2026**. Closures: Thu 24 Dec from 12:00, Sat 26 Dec, Thu 31 Dec from 12:00, Sat 2 Jan 2027.
