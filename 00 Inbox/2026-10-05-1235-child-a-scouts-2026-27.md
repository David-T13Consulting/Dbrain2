## Rules and to-dos
- **Absence:** report in the parents' messaging group (poll; one parent answers) by **Wednesday evening** before the meeting. 3 unreported absences in a term → the place may go to the waiting list.
- **Camp is expected.** An unjustified camp absence bars the child from next year's activities.
- **Snack and drinks rota:** families take turns; when it's ours, plan for **35 people**. The letter assigns the dated slots to other families (ours not seen; check).
- **Activity app (Actiky):** calendar with attendance, documents and authorisations. 2nd-year families: check the details already entered are still correct.
- **Medical form + parental authorisation:** due at the first meeting (19 Sep). Check done.
- **Health-insurance (mutuelle) form:** for children who went to last summer's camp, bring the form for the unit to complete.
- **Younger siblings:** register **before end Jan 2027** to keep priority for Sept 2027.
- **Uniform:** available from the local scout shop.
- Bring cash to events at the hall (no mobile signal for transfers).
