## H. What the pilot taught (for the build brief)
- 95 of 129 Desktop files were iCloud placeholders: the sweep must download first (device-side) or it fails.
- Local OCR (Tesseract, English only) took >3 min for ~100 images and garbles French slides; switch to Apple Vision OCR on the Mac.
- Time-gap grouping gave 53 "sessions", most of them single personal screenshots: grouping needs a learning/not-learning filter first.
- 44% of files were deletable; only 2 sessions were worth a full ingest this week. The weekly review budget holds if the queue is drained one session per week.
- Kept images were unreadable (downscaled to 1400 px, WebP q80). Rule now: crop only, full source resolution, lossless WebP, ≤ 500 KB, check legibility at 100% zoom before proposing. Screen-share captures cap the sharpness: the source is the limit, not the export.
- These learnings are written into `90 System/skills/weekly-desktop-digest/SKILL.md`, which the weekly scheduled task follows.
