    4 · Non-negotiables

    Rules the router never breaks

    Your toggle sets the loosest level

    Data classification, connectors and the client watch-list can only tighten it.

    The question itself is data

    A client name in the question locks the request to L1 or stricter before any text leaves the server.

    No looser fallback

    If the allowed levels cannot answer, the reply says so. It never retries one level looser.

    Whole-vault jobs run at the floor

    Embeddings and wiki maintenance touch every note, so they never leave the strictest level in use.
