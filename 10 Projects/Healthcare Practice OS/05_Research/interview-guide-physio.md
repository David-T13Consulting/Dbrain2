# Interview guide — physiotherapist (kinésithérapeute)

60 minutes. Goal: a quantified map of the administrative day, and which constraints actually hurt.

Mechanics referenced here are verified against nomenclature article 7 — see
[[30 Resources/Belgian Healthcare & eHealth/billing-rules-and-cumulation|billing-rules-and-cumulation]].
Use them to **ask sharper questions**, never to lecture the interviewee.

## 0 · Setup (3 min)

Consent to record, consent to quote, explain that nothing identifying leaves your notes. State plainly
that you are not selling anything.

## 1 · Context (5 min)

- Setting: own practice outside a hospital · practice inside a hospital · organised medical service ·
  home visits · mix. *(These are four different code series, so the mix drives everything.)*
- Solo, group, or part of a multidisciplinary centre? How many practitioners, how many support staff?
- Current tools: record, agenda, billing, accounting. Which are the same tool and which are not?
- Patient mix: *pathologie courante* vs F-list/E-list pathologies, lymphoedema, paediatric/neuro.

## 2 · Walk me through yesterday (15 min)

Chronological, no interruptions for solutions. For each step capture **who, which tool, how many
minutes, how often, what goes wrong**.

Prompts if they stall: first patient of the day → where did the prescription come from → how did you
know they were insured → what did you record during the session → when did you bill it → what happened
at the end of the day → what did you do at the end of the month.

## 3 · The second-session-of-the-day rule (10 min)

This is the richest vein, because the rule is strict and the burden of proof sits on them.

- How often do you see the same patient twice in a day? What kinds of cases?
- **The second session has to be at least 3 hours after the first.** How do you track that? Has a
  session ever been refused or had to be moved because of it?
- Your software bills a different code for the second session. Does it choose that automatically, or do
  you?
- **You have to keep a motivation for why the second session was indispensable, in the patient's file,
  available to the médecin-conseil.** Walk me through the last time you wrote one. How long did it take?
  Where does it live?
- Has a médecin-conseil ever refused reimbursement for a second session? What happened then — who told
  you, how long after, and what did it cost you?

Listen for: whether the justification is written at the time or reconstructed later. Reconstruction is a
risk they are carrying and probably know it.

## 4 · Prescriptions, agreements and caps (8 min)

- Where do prescriptions arrive from, and in what form? Paper, eHealthBox, patient's phone photo?
- How do you handle agreement requests for pathology categories? What happens while you wait?
- For capped treatments — lymphoedema is **120 sessions per calendar year** across code sets — who
  counts? What happens when a patient approaches the cap, and have you ever discovered it too late?
- Paediatric/neuro codes have age and onset conditions requiring a medical report held for the
  médecin-conseil. How do you keep those?

## 5 · Getting paid (10 min)

- Insurability: do you check it, when, and what happens when a patient turns out not to be covered?
- Third-party payer vs patient paying cash — what proportion, and how do you decide per patient?
- **Rejections.** Walk me through the last rejected invoice: how did you learn, what was wrong, how long
  to fix, how much was held up? How often does this happen?
- End of month: what do you reconcile, against what, in what tool, for how long?
- Who chases unpaid patient co-payments?

## 6 · The non-billing admin (5 min)

- Written reports to referring doctors — how many, how long each? *(There is a code for these, and it is
  cumulable with the restricted paediatric codes — do they know and do they bill it?)*
- No-shows, cancellations, waiting list. How is the agenda managed, and by whom?
- Home visits: travel time, what you carry, what you cannot do while out.
- Accountant: what do you hand over, in what format, how often, and how much do you pay?

## 7 · Quantify and close (5 min)

- **If you had to put a number on it: how many hours a week go to administration?** And what would you
  do with that time?
- What have you tried that did not work, and why?
- What would have to be true for you to change software? What would stop you?
- Who decides about software here — you, a manager, a federation recommendation?
- Anything I should have asked?
