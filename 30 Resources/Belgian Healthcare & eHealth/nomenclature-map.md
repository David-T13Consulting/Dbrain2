# Nomenclature map — all articles, all professions

The complete article index of the Belgian *nomenclature des prestations de santé*, with the profession
each governs and its key letter. This is the spine: start here to find which legal text governs a
billing question.

Index page: `inami.fgov.be/fr/nomenclature/nomenclature-textes`, stated as updated through the
*Moniteur belge* of 31 July 2026. **63 articles.** All article texts and 45 interpretative-rule
documents were retrieved and mined on 1 October 2026 — see [[sources]].

## The key letter *is* the profession

Article 1 §2 assigns every service a **key letter** followed by a **nombre-coefficient** expressing its
relative value. Article 1 §3: *"La lettre-clé est un signe dont la valeur en euro est fixée
conventionnellement : cette valeur peut varier pour chacune des lettres-clés."*

So **fee = euro value of the key letter × coefficient**, and the key letter tells you whose scale applies:

| Letter | Scope (art. 1 §2) |
|---|---|
| **N** | Opinions, visits and consultations of any physician or dental practitioner; certain physicians' technical services |
| **K** | Other technical services of doctors of medicine |
| **D** | Disponibilité (availability) |
| **E** | Travel (*déplacement*) of a GP with acquired rights or an accredited GP |
| **B**, **F** | Clinical biology and in-vitro nuclear medicine |
| **A**, **C** | Surveillance by any physician of a **hospitalised** beneficiary |
| **I** | Percutaneous interventional services under medical-imaging control |
| **L** | Technical services of **dental practitioners** |
| **V** | **Midwives** (*accoucheuses*) |
| **M** | **Physiotherapists** (*kinésithérapeutes*) |
| **W** | **Nurses** and nursing personnel |
| **R** | **Speech therapists** (*logopèdes*) |
| **Z** | **Opticians** |
| **S** | **Audiologists** (*acousticiens*) |
| **O** | **Orthopedic technologists** |
| **U** | **Implant suppliers** |
| **Q** | Fee supplement for any **accredited** physician, accredited pharmacist-biologist, or accredited science graduate authorised for clinical biology |

Dental services carry an additional **P — *coefficient de pondération*** (weighting coefficient),
attributed by the AR of 2 June 2015, distinct from the fee letter *(its exact use — to verify)*.

## How to use this

1. Identify the **profession** → key letter → governing article from the tables above.
2. Read that article's **own §§ application paragraphs** for its rules.
3. Read any separate **règles d'application** article (6 dental, 15 surgery, 16 assistance, 19
   radiotherapy, 23 physical medicine).
4. Read the article's ***règles interprétatives*** PDF for resolved edge cases, honouring effective and
   abrogation dates.
5. Check article **1** for the cross-cutting rules, and **26** for urgency/time definitions.
