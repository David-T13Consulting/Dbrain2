# UX in the AI era [2026 update]

Web research on nngroup.com, 1 Oct 2026, summarised in own words. This note supersedes the 2015–17 decks where they differ. Hub: [[30 Resources/NNg UX/README|NN/g hub]]. See also [[ai-trust-evals-and-reliability]] and [[advanced-agents-design-and-teams]].

| Article (NN/g) | Date | Takeaway |
|---|---|---|
| AI Agents as Users (Gibbons, Moran) | 10 Apr 2026 | Agents are users in a functional sense. They read UIs by screenshot (slow, costly), accessibility tree (cheap, rewards semantic HTML) or direct API. Accessibility fundamentals (labels, predictable patterns, semantic markup) now serve humans and agents. Blocking agents can be justified (ad-funded content, regulated friction, platforms that are the AI layer). Long term: agents query APIs while humans keep visual interfaces for comprehension and decisions. |
| The Custodial Era of UX (Kaley, Budiu) | 28 Aug 2026 | AI makes building faster than evaluating, creating UX debt: shipping quickly is not shipping something useful. Fixes: shared judgment to triage can-build vs should-build; faster evaluation (rapid testing, panels, assessment templates); embed design systems, design knowledge and accessibility into generation tools. UX moves from cleanup to prevention. |
| Accelerating Research with AI | 2024, reviewed Jan 2026 | AI helps planning and analysis; cannot observe behaviour; needs intern-level supervision. See [[ux-research-and-usability-testing]]. |
| The UX Reckoning | 10 Jan 2025 | UX job postings about 70% of 2021 levels (2023); only 47% of UX professionals found AI tools valuable; advises going beyond templates, outcome orientation, adjacent skills. |
| Accordion Editing and Apple Picking | 24 Sep 2023 | Early gen-AI chat behaviours: users repeatedly shrink/expand output ("accordion") and re-reference earlier replies ("apple picking"); implication: direct editing, compartmentalised responses, point-and-select. Based on 8 participants. |
| Synthetic Users (NN/g) | 21 Jun 2024 | Useful for desk research, hypotheses and interview guides; unreliable, over-optimistic for decisions or niche groups. Detail in [[personas]]. |
| Evaluating AI-Simulated Behavior | 15 Aug 2025 | Interview-based digital twins beat demographic personas; generic synthetic users show low variability. |

## Implications for David's work
1. Agent-as-user is a design requirement for client digital products (banking, healthcare portals): semantic structure and APIs now matter for non-human users; regulated sectors may choose deliberate friction.
2. UX debt is the governance angle of "AI velocity": pair with eval gates ([[ai-governance-and-compounding]]).
3. Verify dates and claims at source before client use; these are summaries, not quotations.
