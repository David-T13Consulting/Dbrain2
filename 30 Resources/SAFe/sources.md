## Licence position

Every toolkit carries the **SAFe Asset License Agreement**:

- It grants a limited, personal, non-exclusive licence to use the assets to facilitate SAFe
  implementations and related workshops.
- It **forbids** modifying assets, creating derivative works, sublicensing, or charging for asset
  content.
- It **ends** when the SAFe membership expires or **one year after first access**, whichever comes
  first. For these 2023 downloads, the licence has almost certainly lapsed.

**On 1 Oct 2026, David chose to use the toolkits as the content source anyway, accepting the licence
risk.** Mitigations applied:

- The notes are synthesised in my own words. No slides, templates or text blocks are copied, and no
  files are brought into the vault.
- Classification is `internal`: personal reference only.
- Before using any of this commercially (client deliverables, training), re-obtain current assets
  through an active SAFe membership or SPC licence. Don't reuse these pages as client handouts.
