## Part 1, quick capture (with voice step A)
- `POST /capture {text}`: the only write the phone can trigger. It writes **one new file** in `00 Inbox/` named `YYYY-MM-DD-HHMM-<slug>.md`, never edits or overwrites, with frontmatter `classification: internal`, `source: voice-capture`, `review_status: unreviewed` and the four D69 axes. The domain axis (`personal` / `professional`) is never guessed: the Shortcut asks "personal or work?" and David's answer sets it; with no clear answer, nothing is saved (AGENTS.md hard rule 6: unclear classification stops). The other three axes are fixed to wave 1's labels (§4a: ordinary · approved external · private); a capture that would need any other label is refused.
- **Siri entry:** "Hey Siri, tell Brain …" (a second Shortcut, or one Shortcut that routes on "remember/note that" vs. a question). Spoken confirmation; on error, a spoken reason.
- **Guards:** size cap. A capture that looks like client material, mentions a client or an engagement (data-policy §6: the system cannot check that a contract permits David's AI-agent use, so all engagement-related captures are refused), names a child or otherwise breaks §2 or §3a is **refused with a spoken reason and not saved**: nothing prohibited is kept for later triage. When the check is unsure, it refuses. No secrets: a capture containing a token-like string is refused with a spoken reason.
- Git: Inbox captures are listed in `handoff.md` for the lead to commit after a Codex pass, like any other Cowork-side change; only the lead commits, and the runtime never runs git.

## Part 2, digest and update (#10): the S3 safe-write path
Builds on the existing S3 tasks; do not start before **T4.2** (rollback and independent restore tested on a disposable copy).
1. **Source first:** originals to Source_Material with a stable ID (D57) and a pointer note; large files never enter the vault.
2. **Propose, don't write:** the curator produces an exact patch (new notes, edits, links, contradictions) against a base revision, with the evidence for each claim.
3. **Review on the phone:** a short spoken or on-screen summary ("3 new notes, 2 edits, 1 contradiction") with approve / reject / show. Approval binds to that exact patch and revision (P3.2); anything changed since is rejected as stale.
4. **One writer applies it** (P3.3/T4.3): path, schema, link and classification validation, then index/log update; the commit is the lead's, after a Codex pass (Q40). Rollback is one step.
5. **Training materials:** check before ingesting that they are not client-confidential (data policy §2.1, §6). Vendor or public training is fine; a client-run internal training is not.
